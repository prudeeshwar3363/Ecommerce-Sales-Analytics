# Business Insights and Recommendations

## 1. Category Profitability
- Technology generated the highest total profit among the product categories.
- Office Supplies recorded substantial sales and profit.
- Recommendation: Evaluate category-level profit margins before making inventory and marketing decisions.

## 2. Sub-Category Performance
- Copiers recorded the highest total profit.
- Tables generated a significant overall loss.
- Recommendation: Review product costs, discounting, pricing, and return patterns for loss-making sub-categories.

## 3. Regional Performance
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit among the analyzed regions.
- The Central region generated lower profit than the West and East regions.
- Recommendation: Compare regional product mix and profitability to identify opportunities for improvement.

## 4. Discount Analysis
- Some higher discount levels were associated with negative total profit.
- Recommendation: Evaluate discount policies alongside product costs, order size, and sales volume. The observed association does not establish causation.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SQL, Python, and Power BI can be used together to analyze sales performance, identify profitability patterns, and support data-driven business decisions.
